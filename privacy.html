Privacy

Last updated 10 October 2026

Tessera does not collect your data. There are no accounts, cookies, ads or analytics. A few services that make the page work do see your address when you visit, and they are listed below.

What Tessera collects

Nothing. This site has no sign-in, no forms, no tracking scripts and stores nothing in your browser. The Copy buttons write to your clipboard only when you press them, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Services your browser contacts

Tessera is a static page, so these services handle your request. Each one sees your IP address and basic browser details, as any website host does.

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub may keep ordinary server logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces. Your browser requests them from Google when the page loads.
  • The GitHub API is asked for a project's public details, such as when it was last updated, when you open that project's tile.

If you email us

If you write to hello@teseera.tech, we receive your email address and your message through our email provider, Titan. We use them only to reply, and we do not sell or share them. We keep a message for as long as it is needed to answer you.

Links to other sites

Links to GitHub, LinkedIn and other sites lead to services with their own privacy practices. Tessera is not responsible for them.

Changes

If this changes, the date at the top changes with it.
